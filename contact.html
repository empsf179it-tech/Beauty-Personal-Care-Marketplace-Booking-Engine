Frequently Asked Questions

01. How do I modify an existing booking? +

You can modify your booking through the confirmation email link, or by contacting our concierge desk directly at least 24 hours prior to your appointment time.

02. What is your cancellation policy? +

We require a minimum 24-hour notice for all cancellations. Late cancellations or no-shows may be subject to a fee equal to the required deposit.

03. Can I request a specific provider? +

Absolutely. Our booking concierge allows you to browse availability by specific providers or select "Any Available" for maximum flexibility.
